How it works

  1. 1Club & league teams create and publish events in AEM.
  2. 2Fans open events.nfl.com — on the web or inside OnePass and the NFL App.
  3. 3Adobe CDN serves fast pages in the right country and language.
  4. 4Fans register on the hub as a guest, or sign in with NFL ID (same account as OnePass) for one-tap registration.
  5. 5Registration creates the OnePass QR code. Guests get it by email; signed-in fans also see it in My Events with Add to Wallet.
  6. 6Confirmations, reminders and alerts go out through Journey Optimizer and Campaign.
  7. 7Paid events: Buy Tickets opens Ticketmaster — no payments in the hub.
  8. 8Signed-in fans get personalized events and offers (Adobe Target, Real-Time CDP), with their consent. Guests see the standard page.
Target / RT-CDP licences to be confirmed with NFL.
