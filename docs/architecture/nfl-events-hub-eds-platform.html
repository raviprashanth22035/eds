Architecture · NFL Events Hub on EDS

NFL Events Hub — high-level architecture

01 · Club events first

International 1:1 events

Each club authors its own events in its own workspace (per club, per market and language). EDS turns them into the hub, club and event pages with details, venue map and a Register button. Paid events add Buy Tickets to Ticketmaster.

02 · QR registration & entry

Register, then scan at the gate

/api/register stores the registration with the fan’s NFL ID and issues a signed QR (per-club setting). Fans see it in My Events or Wallet. Staff open a sign-in-protected scanner page; /api/checkin validates and marks it used.

03 · Real-time updates

Cancellations reach fans fast

A club sets an event or city to cancelled. Pages show it within seconds via /api/status (short cache) and a republish purges the CDN. Registered fans get email and app push; visitors who opted in get web push.

04 · NFL ID

Public and logged-in views

Log in and Register open id.nfl.com, same as OnePass. Same cached pages; logged-in blocks show my events, status, QR and wallet.

05 · Everywhere

Website, NFL app, OnePass

One build: app mode in NFL app and OnePass WebViews, and an embed mode for nfl.com and club sites. Login inside an embed opens id.nfl.com top-level.

06 · Next

League events & Leap

League-managed events reuse the same blocks. Leap features (badges, scanning, offers, sponsors) plug in behind the middleware later. Styling follows the NFL Global Design Playbook as EDS design tokens.
